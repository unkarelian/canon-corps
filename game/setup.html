System Setup

Canon Corps uses AI models for the town's residents and the entity. They run on your OpenRouter account.

A typical run costs about $0.50 to $1.00. The game also builds the next town in the background, which costs about 10 cents.

Your key is stored on this computer only and is sent only to OpenRouter.

What is OpenRouter?
A service that sells access to many AI models through one account. You add credit there (a few dollars lasts several runs) and the game draws on it as you play. You can see every request and its cost on your OpenRouter activity page, and revoke the game's key at any time.

Connected to OpenRouter.
