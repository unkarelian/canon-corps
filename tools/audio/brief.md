GAME BRIEF. "Canon Corps" is a single-player game played entirely through a fake late-1990s/Y2K government computer desktop (visual vein: Hypnospace Outlaw, Home Safety Hotline). You are a clerk at an agency that "unmakes" lesser realities; you investigate a small town by email, forums and forms while something on the other side slowly notices you. Tone: deadpan bureaucratic comedy that curdles into dread. The music is the agency's own hold/elevator music playing on the work PC. All audio is synthesized by our own simple synth (no samples).

THE AESTHETIC TARGET, in the developer's words: "a light esoteric / cultish influence in its vibe. Like normalcy was tilted just a few degrees off and it's almost ignorable but still weird." The calm music should pass as ordinary office hold music at first listen, yet feel slightly wrong, institutional and quasi-religious if you pay attention. It must NOT sound like a horror score, and must not be generic.
